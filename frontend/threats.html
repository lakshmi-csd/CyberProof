THREAT PULSE

Recent digital safety alerts

These examples show common threat patterns that CyberProof can help users identify.

PHISHING

Fake login requests

Messages may direct users to a fake login page designed to collect credentials.

SAFE ACTION

Verify first

Open the organization's official website directly instead of using the message link.

PAYMENT SCAM

Urgent payment requests

Unexpected requests for money, UPI transfers, or registration fees can require additional verification.

SAFE ACTION

Do not interact

Verify the request through a trusted contact or official channel.

FAKE OFFER

Suspicious rewards

Unexpected prize, cashback, scholarship, or reward claims may use urgency to encourage unsafe actions.

SAFE ACTION

Verify first

Check the organization and offer through an independent official source.
