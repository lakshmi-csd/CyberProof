Digital Safety Learning

Learn how common digital threats work, what warning signs to notice, and what safer action you can take.

01 · PHISHING

Fake login requests

Phishing messages try to make you open a link or provide sensitive information by pretending to be a trusted person or organization.

WARNING SIGNS

Urgent language, unexpected login requests, suspicious links, or requests for passwords and OTPs.

SAFE ACTION

Verify first

Open the official website directly instead of using the message link.

02 · QR SCAMS

Unexpected QR codes

A QR code can lead to a website, payment page, login page, or another destination that you did not expect.

WARNING SIGNS

Unknown QR source, unexpected payment request, or destination different from the expected one.

SAFE ACTION

Check the destination

Confirm where the QR code leads before interacting with it.

03 · PAYMENT SCAMS

Urgent money requests

Scammers may create pressure to send money, make a UPI payment, or pay an unexpected fee.

WARNING SIGNS

Urgency, unexpected payment requests, registration fees, or unusual payment instructions.

SAFE ACTION

Verify independently

Confirm the request using a trusted official contact.

04 · FAKE OFFERS

Rewards and opportunities

Fake offers may promise prizes, cashback, scholarships, internships, or other benefits.

WARNING SIGNS

Unexpected rewards, unrealistic claims, urgency, or requests for money or sensitive details.

SAFE ACTION

Check the source

Verify the organization and offer through an independent official source.
